# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint. Participants should communicate respectfully, accept constructive feedback, and focus disagreements on evidence and ideas.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not tolerated. Do not publish another person's private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub's reporting mechanisms. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban participants based on severity and context. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

This policy is an equivalent formal code inspired by the Contributor Covenant's community standards.
